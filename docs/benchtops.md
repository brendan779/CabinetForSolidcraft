# Benchtops, splashback power points and plinth legs: reference

Research for the owner's answers of 2026-10-26 (BACKLOG BENCH-7, BENCH-8, HAF-1). These numbers
are **defaults**: every one of them is editable in the app, because stones, porcelains and
fabricators differ. Every default is stored with its source and `verified: false` until the owner
or the fabricator confirms it.

## Materials in scope (Australia)

Engineered (crystalline silica) stone has been **banned in Australia since 1 July 2024**. The
benchtop materials are:

- natural stone: granite, marble, **quartzite**
- porcelain / sintered stone: **Zenith Surfaces** and Vasari (Stone Ambassador), Dekton, Neolith,
  Laminam
- laminate on substrate, compact laminate, timber

## Overhangs and supports

There is no single Australian standard. The fabricators' and distributors' published conventions
are below. Our defaults take the Australian sources first.

| Material | Thickness | Unsupported overhang (default) | Beyond that | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Natural stone incl. quartzite | 20 mm | 150 mm | 18 mm substrate up to 300 mm; brackets, legs or a steel frame beyond 300 mm, supports ≤ 600 mm apart | AB Stone (AU) |
| Natural stone incl. quartzite | 30–40 mm | 300 mm | brackets ≤ 600 mm apart | AB Stone (AU); Elite Makeovers (AU) |
| Any stone | any | at most ⅓ of the benchtop's depth | bracing past 300 mm | Natural Stone Institute rule, via Elite Makeovers |
| Zenith Surfaces / Vasari porcelain | 12 mm (on substrate) | 300 mm | steel rods, posts or brackets, by the installer | The Stone People (Stone Ambassador distributor, AU) |
| Porcelain (general) | 12 mm | 300 mm | supports every 600 mm | Archant (NZ); AB Stone (AU) |
| Porcelain (general) | 20 mm | 300 mm | supports every 900 mm | Archant (NZ) |
| Dekton | 12 mm / 20 mm | 250 mm / 500 mm | steel support every 600 mm | Dekton data via worktops.net (UK) |

Quartzite: the Australian sources treat it like other natural stone (above). Overseas fabricators
quote about 150 mm unsupported for 20 mm and 250 mm for 30 mm (Lakeside Surfaces), which is
consistent with the defaults.

## Cut-out clearances (defaults)

| Rule | Default | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sink/cooktop cut-out from the front edge | ≥ 80 mm | AB Stone (AU) |
| Cut-out from the rear edge | ≥ 50 mm | AB Stone (AU) |
| Between sink and tap cut-outs | ≥ 35 mm | AB Stone (AU) |
| Electrical cut-outs from the edge of a surface | ≥ 150 mm | Archant (NZ) |

Sink and cooktop cut-outs are templates with **rectangular** or **radius-corner** shapes (owner,
2026-10-26), sized per appliance and editable.

## Splashback power points (Schneider Electric / Clipsal Iconic)

The owner's standard is a Schneider Electric (Clipsal) set. Defaults for the Clipsal Iconic
horizontal double power point:

| Item | Size | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Complete outlet 3025 (plate) | 120 × 78 mm, 27 mm deep | clipsal.com |
| Grid 3025G | 115 × 71 mm | clipsal.com |
| Mounting centres (blocks and boxes) | 84 mm | Clipsal Iconic mounting accessories datasheet |
| Shallow / deep mounting block | 32 / 40 mm deep | clipsal.com |

The **cut-out** in a stone or porcelain splashback must be hidden by the plate and take the
mounting box behind it. The default is 110 × 68 mm, the plate less 5 mm each side, centred with
84 mm screw centres. Clipsal publishes no splashback template, so the electrician or fabricator
should confirm it. It's editable per job.
